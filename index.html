What we do

Stockton Labs builds an AI phone receptionist for home-services businesses — plumbers, HVAC, and other trades. When a call to your business goes unanswered, our system answers, captures the caller's details, and texts you the lead so you can call back fast. No sales team, no contracts.

SMS notifications

Customers who sign up receive SMS lead notifications from Stockton Labs. Message frequency varies with call volume. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Customers opt in by signing our pilot order form. We never sell or share mobile opt-in information with third parties or for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
