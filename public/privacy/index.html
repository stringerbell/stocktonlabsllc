Overview

Stockton Labs LLC ("Stockton Labs," "we," "us") provides software services to businesses, including an AI phone receptionist service. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. It applies to our website (stocktonlabsllc.com) and our services.

Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect:

From our customers. Account and contact details such as your name, business information, email address, mobile phone number, and billing information.

Data we process on your behalf. To deliver our services, we may collect and process information that you or your end users provide — which, depending on the service, can include names, phone numbers, addresses, the content of communications, and recordings and transcripts of calls. We process this information on behalf of, and at the direction of, our business customers.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide, operate, support, and improve our services; to communicate with you, including SMS notifications and service messages where you have opted in; and to bill for our services.

SMS / Text Messaging

When you sign up and provide your mobile number, you consent to receive SMS notifications and service messages from Stockton Labs related to the service you have signed up for.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile opt-in information, phone numbers, or consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information may be shared only with the service providers that help us deliver the messaging (for example, our telephony/SMS provider), strictly for that purpose and not for their own marketing.

Message frequency. For our AI phone receptionist service, you will receive a text each time the service captures a new lead, plus occasional service messages; frequency varies with your call volume.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help.

How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers (such as telephony/SMS, voice-AI, and cloud-hosting vendors) that need it to operate our services on our behalf, under agreements that limit their use of it; when required by law; or to protect our legal rights. We do not sell personal information. As stated above, mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared for third-party marketing.

Communications and Recordings

Depending on the service, communications such as phone calls may be recorded and transcribed in order to provide the service to our customers.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide our services and as required by law, and we use reasonable measures to protect it.

Your Choices

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. To request access to or deletion of your information, contact us at dann@stocktonlabsllc.com.

Children's Privacy
